# F24 — Tyre-model repair and headline retrain

Depends on: F02, F08, F09, F14, F18 (all Done)
Spec anchors: 5.4, 5.5.2, 5.5.3, 5.6.1, 5.8.2, 5.12, T5, E-10, E-15, F-16, F-20, A-09, AR-18, R-12, M1–M11

> Extends master spec v3.0 rather than implementing a row of it, on the F18 precedent.
> The anchors are the contracts the repair must not break — the T5 transfer discipline,
> E-10's identification honesty, the mask-based compliance mechanism — while the
> degradation constants underneath them are expected to move at most circuits.

## Goal

No compound the simulator serves is ever free. Today 37 of 145 fitted degradation
curves carry `a = 0.0` — the 2026 SOFT at nearly every circuit, all three compounds
at Canada — because the fitter's `a >= 0` bound clips flat residuals to zero and the
zero is stored as a measurement. The T5 ratio transfer then propagates it: a donor
pool of zeros transfers zero, and `target_2024_a / donor_2024_a` zeroes anything it
touches besides. The frozen policy learned the exploit (pit lap 2–3, one set to the
flag) and M9 measured it at 37.9 tyre failures per 100 races against a bar of 2.
After F24 a bound-clip is stored as *unidentified*, repaired curves carry their
source, the env refuses to serve a zero, and the headline is retrained under the
repaired physics with the full M1–M11 suite re-run against the unchanged bars.

## Deliverables

- `senna/physics/fits/degradation.py` — bound-clip detection (`identified` on the
  fit), pooled per-compound refit over fractional residuals, cross-era prior.
- `senna/physics/fits/run.py` — the repair pass between degradation and tyre_style;
  transfer donors restricted to identified fits; params emission carries
  `identified` and `source`.
- `senna/env/scenario.py` — the no-free-tyres backstop: zero-deg slicks leave
  `available_compounds` (and therefore the F-16 mask); the two-compound refusal
  reads `min_dry_compounds` from configs instead of an inline 2.
- `configs/fits.yaml` — the repair constants (below).
- `configs/env.yaml` — `min_dry_compounds`.
- `configs/physics_params.yaml` — regenerated by `make fit`; never hand-edited.
- `models/headline`, `models/era2024`, `models/explain` — re-rolled via the
  existing targets; new model version throughout (E-15).
- `docs/results/F14-pre-repair/` — the pre-repair store, archived for the
  before/after comparison; `docs/results/F14/` re-rolled and `make report` rerun.
- `tests/physics/test_deg_repair.py`, `tests/env/test_scenario_guards.py`.

## Out of scope

The inverted compound offsets (reported in the fit diff, not repaired); any reward
change (5.5.4 is spec-locked at three terms); failure-hazard re-pricing; UI work;
the flaky tests/api slow tests INDEX tracks.

## Constants & configs

- `degradation.signal_floor_frac_of_s_ref: 0.5` — operational: a curve whose
  predicted delta at its own max observed age is under half the circuit's clean-lap
  sigma is indistinguishable from lap noise over the range the data covers, so it
  is no signal — `configs/fits.yaml`.
- `race.min_dry_compounds: 2` — sourced: the F-20 two-compound rule is
  unsatisfiable below it — `configs/env.yaml`.

## Tests first

- a fit whose amplitude lands on the bound is stored `identified: false`
- a fit above the signal floor keeps `identified: true`
- the pooled refit recovers known wear planted in synthetic fractional residuals
- an unidentified circuit fit is replaced by the pooled curve, `source: "pooled"`
- pooled-unidentified falls through to the cross-era prior, `source: "prior_cross_era"`
- transfer donor pools exclude unidentified fits
- property: for every circuit-era build_scenario serves, every compound in
  `available_compounds` has `deg_a > 0` (run 491 as an invariant)
- a slick with `a = 0` in params is excluded from `available_compounds`
- a circuit left under `min_dry_compounds` refuses loudly
- regression by name: australian_grand_prix / 2026 / SOFT, deg at age 55 > 0
- the F16 golden engine fixtures still pass untouched (pinned configs — the engine
  did not move, only the params)
