# 5.4 Synthetic 2026 generation — eight named transformations

Extracted from the SENNA Master Implementation Specification v3.0 (7 July 2026) — docs/spec/source/SENNA_Master_Spec_v3.pdf, Part 5. The PDF is the authority; report divergences, never patch them here.

Purpose: produce 2026-era race dynamics for every circuit, including those the 2026 season has not visited yet. Method: transform fitted 2024 behaviour with sourced physics deltas, then calibrate the residual against real 2026 rounds 1-6 and validate on rounds 7+ (A-02, E-14). "No GAN" stands: every transformation is a formula you can defend line by line.

| ID | Transformation | Formula / procedure | Rationale and source |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Powertrain reprofile | Recompute straight-line speed profiles with a point-mass longitudinal model: P(v) = P_ICE + P_K(v) with P_K = 350 kW to 290 km/h tapering to 0 at 355; drag from T2. Output per circuit: full-throttle time, terminal speeds, deployable energy integral E_dep. | FIA power/taper figures (5.2). This is what converts telemetry into 2026 energy budgets. |
| T2 | Aero scaling | Straights: drag force x0.45 in X-mode sections (source: about -55% drag). Corners: apparent grip from downforce x0.70 passed through the corner-speed model (v_corner scales roughly with the fourth root of downforce at fixed radius; fitted, not assumed). | Honda 2026 overview; fitted exponent documented in the calibration notebook. |
| T3 | Mass shift | Minimum mass 768 kg; lap-time mass sensitivity k_m fitted per circuit from the joint fuel/evolution regression (E-10), applied to the -30 kg delta and the new fuel profile. | Honda 2026 overview; avoids v2's fixed 0.035 s/kg. |
| T4 | Energy accounting layer | Per circuit: H_max from config (default 8.5 MJ); harvest-by-mode = braking integral + mode-dependent lift-and-coast term with pace cost c_lc (s/MJ) and clipping term; E_dep from T1. These four numbers per circuit are the entire energy game. | F-02/F-04 corrections; Raceteq/Autosport mechanism reporting. |
| T5 | Tyre compounds 2026 | Refit degradation curves grip(age) = 1 - a*age^b per compound on real 2026 rounds 1-6; for circuits without 2026 data, transfer coefficients from the nearest cluster (clustering on 2022-2024 abrasiveness + layout features), flagged low-confidence. | Pirelli 2026 compounds differ; measured beats guessed. |
| T6 | Fuel/energy budget | Initial fuel mass from the race energy allowance (Conf. C — verify); burn profile scaled so total burn = allowance; weight effect through T3. | F-06 correction. |
| T7 | Overtaking model | Pass probability P(pass \| pace delta, override available) refit on 2026 rounds 1-6 overtake counts; prior: override is worth roughly 0.1-0.2 s (vs DRS 0.6-0.8 s), so passing is harder than 2024. | ESPN 2026 guide; fitted, then checked against round 7+ overtake counts. |
| T8 | Residual recalibration | After T1-T7, per calibration circuit fit the leftover offset c_circuit = median(real 2026 lap time) - median(simulated); apply; report held-out error on rounds 7+ as the headline sim-to-real number. | Standard model-correction step; keeps all physics terms interpretable while absorbing unmodelled effects into one named constant. |

## 5.4.1 Validation targets for the generator (reported in Chapter 6)

- Lap-time distribution: Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance between simulated and real 2026 green-lap distributions per held-out circuit; target KS under 0.15, reported per circuit.
- Stint lengths: simulated vs real stint-length histograms per compound; report earth-mover distance and comment on outliers.
- Pit loss: simulated pit delta within 1.0 s of the OpenF1-measured mean per circuit.
- Event rates: SC/VSC per race within the 2022-2026 binomial confidence band per circuit.
- Energy plausibility: simulated derate frequency qualitatively consistent with 2026 broadcast/reporting (cars running dry on straights at energy-hard circuits).
