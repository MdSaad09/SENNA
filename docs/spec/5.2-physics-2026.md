# 5.2 Corrected 2026 physics model (the constants that drive everything)

Extracted from the SENNA Master Implementation Specification v3.0 (7 July 2026) — docs/spec/source/SENNA_Master_Spec_v3.pdf, Part 5. The PDF is the authority; report divergences, never patch them here.

These replace v2 Section 4.5. Column "Conf." is the confidence grade from the Assumptions Register (Part 8): A = verified against two or more primary sources; B = single good source; C = estimate to be fitted or verified before use.

| Quantity | 2024 era | 2026 era | Source / note | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MGU-K max power | 120 kW | 350 kW | FIA 2026 PU regs Art. 5; Formula1.com explainers | A |
| ICE power (approx.) | 550-560 kW | ~400 kW | Formula1.com / ESPN 2026 guides | A |
| Deployment speed taper | n/a | full 350 kW to 290 km/h, linear to 0 at 355 km/h | Formula1.com 2026 PU explainer | A |
| Manual Override (replaces DRS as overtaking aid) | DRS flap | 350 kW held to 337 km/h, ~0.5 MJ extra, within 1.0 s at detection | Formula1.com; ESPN | A |
| Energy Store usable capacity | 4 MJ | 4 MJ (unchanged) | ESPN 2026 rules guide; FIA Art. 5.4 (cross-check) | A |
| Harvest cap per lap | ~2 MJ (MGU-K) | 8.5 MJ baseline; FIA-adjusted ~5-9 MJ per event; may be lower in qualifying | FIA regs + Raceteq / Autosport 2026 reporting | A |
| Harvest mechanisms | braking (+MGU-H) | braking; lift-and-coast at 350 kW (disables X-mode); super clipping at ~250 kW on throttle | Raceteq May 2026; Autosport Mar 2026 | B |
| MGU-H | present | abolished | FIA 2026 regs | A |
| Active aero | none (DRS only) | X-mode (low drag, designated sections) / Z-mode (high downforce); available to all cars | Formula1.com aero explainer | A |
| Downforce / drag vs 2024 | baseline | downforce about -30%, drag about -55% | Honda 2026 regulations overview | B |
| Minimum car mass | ~798 kg | 768 kg | Honda 2026 overview (-30 kg) | A |
| Race fuel mass | ~100 kg | ~70 kg (energy-flow limited, 100% sustainable) | VERIFY vs FIA 2026 regs before Phase 2 | C |
| Fastest-lap point | existed to 2024 | abolished from 2025 | VERIFY vs 2026 Sporting Regs (30-second check) | B |
| Grid size | 20 cars | 22 cars (11 teams incl. Cadillac) | 2026 season fact | A |
| 2026 calendar | n/a | 22 rounds after two cancellations; Madrid new in September; ~10 rounds raced by 7 July 2026 | Sky Sports / season reporting | A |

Design consequence: the strategic tension of 2026 is a 4 MJ tank feeding a 350 kW motor, refilled only by spending lap time. Every layer of v3 — actions, observation, physics, visualisation, metrics — is organised around SOC scarcity rather than the fictional per-lap cap.
