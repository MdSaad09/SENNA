# 5.7 Curriculum — three stages with competence gates

Extracted from the SENNA Master Implementation Specification v3.0 (7 July 2026) — docs/spec/source/SENNA_Master_Spec_v3.pdf, Part 5. The PDF is the authority; report divergences, never patch them here.

| Stage | Environment | Exit gate (evaluated on frozen seed sets) | Why this gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — solo fundamentals | One circuit (Monza), dry, deterministic weather, no rivals, full 2026 energy model | On 100 fixed seeds: (i) eval return improvement under 1% across three consecutive evals (plateau) AND (ii) mean race time within 1.5% of the scripted solo reference (grid search over 1–3 stop plans x greedy energy schedule) | A solo race has a computable near-optimal reference, so competence is measured against an objective bar, not against the agent's own history |
| B — competition | Six circuits (Monza, Barcelona, Silverstone, Spa, Suzuka, Monaco — kept from v2, OQ-7), 21 rivals with mixed archetypes, dry, SC/VSC on | On 200 paired seeds vs the rule-based baseline in identical stochastic draws: finish-ahead rate at least 55% AND median position delta at least 0 | Paired seeds cancel safety-car luck, so the gate isolates decision quality (A-06); 55% requires real superiority beyond noise |
| C — full problem | All circuits with validated data, weather transitions, red flags, stochastic rival parameter draws (train band only, E-06), era 2026 | Stop when eval mean points stagnates for 1M steps; select the checkpoint by validation-seed points; report only on held-out test seeds, held-out rival band and held-out 2026 rounds | Standard train/validation/test discipline, applied to RL evaluation |

Step budgets from 5.6.1 are ceilings, not schedules (F-21). Gate values are logged to curriculum_gates.json — the interpretability contract row for this component. VecNormalize statistics carry across stages (a documented choice: continuity beats re-warming for an observation vector whose bounds do not change).
