# 5.10 Frontend — six views, each with its assumptions stated

Extracted from the SENNA Master Implementation Specification v3.0 (7 July 2026) — docs/spec/source/SENNA_Master_Spec_v3.pdf, Part 5. The PDF is the authority; report divergences, never patch them here.

## 5.10.1 Circuit ribbon algorithm (replaces v2 Section 4.4 — see F-12, E-17)

1. Pick the fastest clean lap of the most recent dry session for the circuit; take get_pos_data() X, Y, Z; convert decimetres to metres.
2. Resample the centreline by arc length at 2 m spacing; close the loop; smooth X, Y and (separately, harder) Z with a Savitzky-Golay filter.
3. Build rotation-minimizing frames along the curve (parallel transport) — Frenet frames twist at inflection points and hairpins (E-17).
4. Extrude a 12 m ribbon; clamp half-width to local curve radius to prevent self-intersection; assert no self-intersection in the geometry unit test for every circuit.
5. Elevation: smoothed Z exaggerated x1.5, labelled stylised in the UI; where Z is missing, flat with corner camber — also labelled. Banking is a visual treatment, not data (F-12).
6. Annotate: corners from get_circuit_info(); braking zones from speed-drop detection on telemetry (drop above 50 km/h within 100 m — kept from v2); X-mode sections as the longest full-throttle stretches (stylised until official section data is available); pit lane as an offset spline.
7. Export GLTF + metadata JSON per circuit; lap length must match the official figure within 5% (kept from v2).

## 5.10.2 The six views: purpose, key change vs v2, and where each is brittle

| View | Kept purpose + change vs v2 | Brittleness / assumptions to state |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Circuit Explorer | As v2; "active aero zones" relabelled X-mode sections; energy annotations show harvest zones + per-circuit H_max | Ribbon is stylised in elevation and banking; X-mode sections are inferred, not official — both stated in the legend |
| 2 Race Replay | As v2 with 22 cars via one InstancedMesh (per-instance colour); labels only for selected car + top 3 (F-28); generic liveries (F-29) | Car positions between laps are spline-interpolated along track distance — mid-lap battles are illustrative, and the UI says so |
| 3 Energy View (was "ERS Landscape") | Rebuilt: SOC ribbon over laps + paired harvest/deploy bars against the per-circuit harvest cap line; the fictional 4 MJ deploy plane is gone (F-02) | Energy series are simulator-internal (F-13) — footer states "modelled, calibrated to 2026 aggregate behaviour" |
| 4 Tyre Surface | As v2 (grip over age x temperature, vertex-shader surface, cliff visible) | Surface renders the fitted model, not raw data; compounds without 2026 fits show the transfer-cluster flag (T5) |
| 5 Live Simulation | As v2 but explicitly the agent in the simulator (R-05); decision card panel streams per lap with fidelity badge | Playback clock decouples sim speed from animation (E-16); WebGL context-loss overlay (E-18) |
| 6 Counterfactual | As v2 (split viewport, synced lap clock, divergence flash, delta chart) — era-matched runs (F-24) | Header states era config and "in-simulator comparison under stated assumptions"; the honesty is the credibility |

## 5.10.3 Performance budget (measured, not hoped)

| Budget item | Limit | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Canvases mounted at once | 1 | Views are routes; navigating unmounts (F-28) |
| Draw calls in replay | under 150 | InstancedMesh for cars; merged ribbon geometry; LOD beyond 500 m (kept from v2) |
| DOM (Drei Html) labels | at most 4 | Selected car + podium; others use instanced sprite text |
| Postprocessing | bloom only, toggleable | Quality switch defaults off below a GPU heuristic; kept from v2's toggle idea |
| Frame rate | 60 fps target / 45 fps floor on a GTX 1660-class GPU | Chrome performance trace of a full 70-lap replay is a Phase 7 exit artifact |
